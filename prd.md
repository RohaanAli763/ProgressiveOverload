# Product Requirements Document

## Product Summary
ProgressiveOverload is a mobile app for tracking gym sessions with a focus on progression over time. It helps users remember what they lifted last time, what reps they completed, and how their workouts evolved across different training days.

## Problem Statement
Gym sessions rely on memory or notes apps that are slow to update and hard to compare over time. Users need a faster way to log sets and revisit their workout history so they can progressively overload with confidence.

## Goals
- Track workout days such as leg day, push day, pull day, arm day, and custom days.
- Store exercises under each day.
- Record weight, reps, and future set-level details.
- Show previous workout history for quick comparison.
- Make editing past workout entries fast and convenient on mobile.

## Target Users
- Gym-goers who follow structured routines.
- Users who want a simple historical view of their performance.
- People who want less friction than a generic notes app.

## MVP Scope
- Authentication with Supabase.
- Workout day grouping.
- Exercise list per workout day.
- Session logging for weight and reps.
- History view for previous sessions.
- Quick edit flow for recent logs.

## Non-Goals for the First Version
- Social features.
- AI-based workout planning.
- Detailed nutrition tracking.
- Wearable device integrations.

## Success Criteria
- Users can log a session in under a minute.
- Users can see their previous performance for each exercise.
- Users can update logged weight and reps without losing history.
